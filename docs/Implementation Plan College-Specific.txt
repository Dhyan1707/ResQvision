Phase 4: AI Model Training (Dataset & Architecture)
Depending on your answer to the open question regarding what we are training, here is the general approach for an Evacuation Route Optimization Model (Reinforcement Learning):

Dataset Requirements
To train an AI model for this, we don't necessarily need external datasets; we can generate synthetic data using our simulation.

Inputs (State):
Current location of the victim (X, Y, Z / Node ID).
Locations of active fires (List of Node IDs).
Building graph (Rooms and connections).
Outputs (Action):
Next node to move to.
Rewards:
+100 for reaching an exit.
-100 for entering a fire node.
-1 for each step taken (to encourage the shortest path).
Training Plan
Environment Sandbox: We will build a headless (no UI) version of our building graph to run thousands of simulations rapidly.
Algorithm: Implement Q-Learning or a Deep Q-Network (DQN) using TensorFlow.js or a Python backend.
Training Loop: The agent will spawn in random rooms with random fires and learn the building layout by trial and error.
Integration: Replace routeGenerator.js with the trained model's inference engine to predict the next best step in real-time.
Verification Plan
Manual Verification
3D Rendering: Verify that the college layout is correctly rendered in 3D in the dashboard.
Simulation: Click a room to start a fire, verify that the 3D route is drawn correctly away from the fire.
Agent Logic: Spawn a victim and observe them following the 3D path to safety.
Automated Tests
Graph connectivity tests: Ensure all nodes in buildingData.js have a valid path to an exit when no fires are present.
Route validity tests: Ensure routeGenerator.js never outputs a path that includes a fire node.

How the 3D Model, Person, AI Agent, and Evacuation Route work together:
Here is the exact step-by-step pipeline of your project:

Step 1: Make the 3D Model (buildingData.js + ThreeDViewer.js)
Building Graph: All 8 floors of your college (Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic) are modeled as 3D floor plates and room nodes (Classrooms, Computer Labs, Chemistry Labs, Staircases, Passageways, and Ground Exits).
WebGL Rendering: ThreeDViewer.js takes this building graph and programmatically extrudes and positions 3D room blocks in WebGL.
Step 2: Create the Person / Victim (victimAgent.js)
When you click 👤 Spawn Victim, VictimAgent.js instantiates a 3D humanoid/sphere mesh inside a specific room (e.g. 5th Floor E-Library).
Step 3: Ignite Fire (fireSimulationEngine.js)
When you click 🔥 Ignite Fire, the fire engine places a 3D fire sphere on a room (e.g. 5th Floor Computer Lab 1) and blocks connected paths.
Step 4: AI Agent Evacuation Routing (rlEvacuationEnv.js + routeGenerator.js)
When you click 🚀 Start Evacuation, the AI Agent (Reinforcement Learning Q-Learning model) evaluates the active fire hazard, finds the shortest safe node path to the ground floor main entrance, and plots a glowing 3D safe route line.
The Victim (victimAgent.js) animates frame-by-frame along those 3D waypoints until safely exiting the building.
